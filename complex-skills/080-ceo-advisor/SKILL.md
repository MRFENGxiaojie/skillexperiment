## Scope and Limitations

- Provides leadership frameworks, processes, and decision support — it is not legal, financial, tax, HR-compliance, or fiduciary advice, and does not replace counsel in regulated matters (M&A, securities, employment, governance filings).
- Works only from company data the user actually provides: metrics, financials, market figures, and history must come from the user or upstream analysis — never invent or default-fill them, and label every assumption as such.
- Frameworks are general management practice that must be adapted to the company's jurisdiction, industry, stage, and governance structure; they do not substitute for the board's, shareholders', or owners' actual decision authority.
- Produces analyses, plans, and drafts — not communications sent on the user's behalf. Board packages, investor updates, and crisis statements are reviewed and approved by the user before use.
- Covers founder to mid-market executive practice; public-company obligations (earnings calls, SEC filings, disclosure regimes) are out of scope.

## Output Format

Deliverables per request:

- **Strategic planning**: a strategy analysis report (market/competitive position, options, risks, roadmap, recommendations), with the data sources and assumptions used stated alongside each conclusion.
- **Financial scenarios**: risk-adjusted scenario projections (base/upside/downside) with the key assumptions driving each case, from `scripts/financial_scenario_analyzer.py` or an equivalent.
- **Decision support**: the completed decision checklist (problem defined, data gathered, options evaluated, stakeholders consulted, risks assessed, implementation planned, success metrics defined, communication prepared) for any go/no-go decision.
- **Board and investor materials**: board package outline per the T-4 to T-0 timeline, board meeting agenda, investor update structure, and fundraising/pitch materials.
- **Culture and organization**: culture transformation timeline (assessment → design → implementation → embedding) with key levers, and a succession planning timeline where requested.

All outputs separate user-provided data from framework-based guidance; no fabricated company metrics.
