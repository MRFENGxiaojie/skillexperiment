# Practice-Area Templates (Spec)

> Mandatory reading before classifying any issue. Precedence: plugin clinic
> config (`legal-clinic/CLAUDE.md`) when loaded → this file → the built-in
> Step 2 defaults in SKILL.md. When a clinic config is loaded, its templates
> override this file; when not, use this file's question sets and red flags.

## Immigration

**Questions:**
- Current status and how entered
- Any prior applications, removals, encounters with ICE/CBP
- Country conditions relevant to any asylum/withholding claim
- Family members and their statuses
- Criminal history (sensitive — explain why asking)
- Timeline urgency: pending hearings, deadlines, NTAs

**Red flags:** pending removal proceedings; detention; NTA or ICE check-in
date; employer retaliation threats; DACA/humanitarian status at risk.

## Housing

**Questions:**
- Type of housing (private, subsidized, public)
- What happened: notice received, lockout, conditions problem, deposit dispute
- Lease terms and payment history
- Habitability issues (repairs requested, landlord response, documentation)
- Timeline urgency: notice date, court date if any

**Red flags:** eviction summons or hearing date; self-help eviction
(lockout/utility cutoff); landlord retaliation (calling ICE, threats);
substandard conditions with uninhabitable-unit indicators.

## Family

**Questions:**
- Relationship and what's at issue (custody, support, divorce, protection)
- Children involved — ages, current arrangement
- Safety: any violence, threats, fear (handle carefully — see cross-area flags)
- Existing court orders
- Timeline urgency: any hearings scheduled

**Red flags:** DV indicators (threats, escalating behavior, prior incidents);
ex parte or emergency order windows; child abduction risk.

## Consumer

**Questions:**
- Type of debt or dispute
- Who's contacting them and how (FDCPA relevance)
- Documentation: contracts, statements, collection letters
- Has anything been filed against them
- Timeline urgency: answer deadlines, garnishment, judgment

**Red flags:** wage garnishment in progress or imminent; answer deadline in
days; debt collector violations of FDCPA/state equivalents.

## Cross-area spotting

While running any template, flag issues outside the routed area:

| Client says | Also flags |
|---|---|
| "I'm worried about my immigration status" | Immigration — even in a housing intake |
| "My partner [threatening behavior]" | DV / family law / protective order |
| "I can't work because of my injury" | Possible benefits/disability claim |
| "They're taking money from my paycheck" | Garnishment — consumer/employment overlap |
| "The landlord said he'd call ICE" | Housing + immigration + possible retaliation |

Every cross-area issue is noted in the summary; the clinic's decision to
handle or refer is the professor's call.

## Conflict-check minimums

- Opposing party name(s) — does the clinic represent or have represented them?
- Related parties — anyone else the student or clinic might have a conflict with?
- Positional conflicts — would this case hurt another clinic client?

Surface flags for professor review. Do not resolve conflicts.
