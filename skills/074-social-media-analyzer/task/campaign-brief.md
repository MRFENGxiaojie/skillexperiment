# Campaign Background: July New Product Promotion Campaign

## Campaign Goals
- Build buzz for the launch of the new product (light-meal energy bars) and raise brand awareness and engagement
- Drive users to click the product link to view details (in-app + standalone site)
- Accumulate content and audience data for the official on-sale launch next month (August)

## Basic Information
- Campaign period: 2026-07-01 to 2026-07-31
- Platform: Instagram (organic posts + a small amount of paid promotion)
- Total ad budget: CNY 5,000 (RMB)
- Published content: 8 posts (2 image posts, 2 carousels, 3 Reels, 1 Story)

## Target Audience
- People aged 18-35 interested in fitness / light meals / fat loss / healthy eating
- Main cities: first-tier and new first-tier cities
- Ad targeting: interest tags "fitness", "light meals", "healthy living"

## Next Month's Decision Points
- Whether to add more budget (management leans toward increasing it to CNY 10K-15K)
- Which content and formats to reuse
