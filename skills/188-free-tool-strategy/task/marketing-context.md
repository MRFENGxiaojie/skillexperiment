# Marketing Context

Context file for free tool work. The agent reads this before asking questions and only asks about what's not covered.

## Product and Audience

- Core product and who buys it: payroll outsourcing + benefits management SaaS, ~$15K/yr ACV, target customers are HR directors at small/mid businesses with 100–500 employees.
- Problem the ideal customer has that a free tool could solve adjacently: HR needs to quickly estimate take-home pay, compare benefit costs, and assess their own compliance status — these questions all come up before they talk to a vendor.
- What the audience searches for that isn't the product: search terms like "US paycheck calculator", "employee benefits cost", "HR compliance checklist", "take-home pay calculation".

## Resources

- Engineering time available: 1 backend + 0.5 frontend, ~2 weeks available per quarter.
- Design resources: a small amount of design resource.
- Who maintains the tool after launch: no dedicated ops; 1 marketer maintains it part-time.

## Goals

- Primary goal: SEO traffic + lead (MQL) capture + organic backlinks.
- Definition of a win: 200 MQLs per quarter, 30 organic backlinks.
- SEO status: 80K monthly organic visits, all from blog, no tool pages.

## Competitors

- 2 decent free paycheck calculators already exist (e.g., PaycheckCity).
- Company benefits cost comparator: no clear free alternative, but search volume to be validated.
- HR compliance checklist: no good free alternative.
