# Customer Information Protection Measures - Key Points Summary (new regulation)

> This document is a key-points summary (not the official full text) of the new regulation "Customer Information Protection Measures", compiled by the compliance team. The officially published text prevails. Effective date: 2026-10-01.

## Summary content (8 requirements)

1. **Minimal necessary collection**: Financial institutions should limit collection of customer information to the necessary scope directly related to business; over-scope collection is prohibited; the necessity of the information should be assessed before carrying out the business.
2. **Notice and consent**: Before collecting customer information, institutions should notify the purpose, method, scope, and retention period of collection in a prominent way and obtain the customer's explicit consent; consent should be revocable.
3. **Special protection for sensitive information**: Collection and processing of sensitive information such as ID numbers, financial accounts, and biometrics requires separate consent and enhanced safeguards such as encrypted storage.
4. **Storage and retention periods**: Customer information must not be retained longer than business needs; after the retention period expires or the business terminates, the data should be deleted or anonymized; retention beyond the committed period is prohibited.
5. **Rights of access and correction**: Customers have the right to query and copy their personal information and to request corrections; institutions should respond within the prescribed time limit (the recommended response time for individual-rights requests is 15 working days; the final text prevails).
6. **Transparency of automated decision-making**: Where customer information is used for automated decision-making (e.g., credit-limit assessment, risk-control scoring), institutions should disclose the decision basis and main logic to the customer and provide a channel for human review.
7. **Security-incident reporting**: If a security incident such as a leak of customer information occurs, institutions should report to the regulator within 24 hours of discovery and notify affected customers as required.
8. **Internal systems and training**: Institutions should establish a sound customer-information-protection system, define role responsibilities and approval processes, and conduct regular training and assessment of staff.
