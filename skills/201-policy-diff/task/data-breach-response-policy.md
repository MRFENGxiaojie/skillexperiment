This is the current official version of the company's Data Breach Response
Policy. It applies to all business units, subsidiaries, and employees, and to
third parties that process personal information on the company's behalf.

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose

The purpose of this Policy is to define how the company detects, assesses,
contains, and responds to a Personal Information Breach, and to coordinate any
notification that applicable law requires.

### 1.2 Scope

This Policy applies to all personal information handled by the company,
regardless of medium, and to breaches affecting customers, employees,
contractors, suppliers, and business contacts.

---

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Personal Information Breach

**Personal Information Breach** means the accidental or unlawful destruction,
loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, personal
information.

### 2.2 Breach response team

The **Breach Response Team** is convened by the Privacy Office and includes
representatives from Legal, IT Security, Customer Operations, and
Communications.

---

## 3. Detection and Initial Assessment

### 3.1 Detection

All employees and contractors must report suspected breaches or suspected
unauthorized access to personal information to the Privacy Office
immediately upon becoming aware of them.

### 3.2 Initial assessment

The Privacy Office assesses each reported incident to determine whether it is
a Personal Information Breach, its scope, the types of information affected,
and the risk of harm to affected individuals. An incident that is not a
Personal Information Breach may still be handled under IT incident-management
procedures.

---

## 4. Containment and Investigation

### 4.1 Containment

The Breach Response Team takes immediate steps to contain the breach, including
isolating affected systems, revoking compromised credentials, and preserving
evidence for investigation.

### 4.2 Investigation

The team investigates the cause and scope of the breach, documents the
timeline and root cause, and prepares an internal incident record.

---

## 5. Notification

### 5.1 General

Where applicable law requires notification, the company notifies the affected
individuals and the relevant regulator in accordance with the law of the
applicable jurisdiction.

### 5.2 Content of notification

Notifications include, where applicable: a description of the breach, the types
of information involved, the measures taken to contain and mitigate the breach,
and guidance for affected individuals on protecting themselves.

### 5.3 Timing

The company completes notifications as soon as practicable and within any
deadline that applicable law imposes. Internal escalation to the Privacy
Office occurs immediately upon detection as set out in Section 3.1.

---

## 6. Documentation and Post-Incident Review

### 6.1 Incident records

The Privacy Office maintains a record of each breach, its assessment, the
actions taken, and the notifications made.

### 6.2 Post-incident review

After each material breach, the Breach Response Team conducts a post-incident
review, identifies root causes and corrective actions, and tracks the actions
to closure.

---

## 7. Governance and Review

### 7.1 Accountability

The Head of Privacy owns this Policy and is accountable for its implementation.

### 7.2 Review

This Policy is reviewed at least annually and after any material breach.
Changes require approval by the Privacy Council and Legal.
