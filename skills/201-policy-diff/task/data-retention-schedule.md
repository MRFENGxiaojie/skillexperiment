This Schedule documents how long the company retains personal information and
records in each business area. It supplements Section 5 (Data Retention) of the
Personal Information Protection Policy.

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Schedule is to set consistent retention periods so that
records are kept no longer than the business and legal need for them.

---

## 2. Retention periods

| Record category | Retention period | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Customer account records | 5 years after account closure | Business records |
| Customer transaction records | 7 years from the transaction date | Tax and audit requirements |
| Customer support and complaint records | 3 years from the last contact | Business records |
| Marketing and consent records | 2 years after the most recent consent | Business records |
| Employee personnel records | 7 years after termination of employment | Labor and tax requirements |
| Security and access logs | 1 year from the log date | IT security operations |

Retention periods above are the maximum periods for which the records are
kept in the ordinary course of business.

---

## 3. End-of-retention handling

### 3.1 Archive

Records that have ongoing legal or audit value may be archived in accordance
with the records-management standard maintained by the Records Office.

### 3.2 Deletion

When a record reaches the end of its retention period and is not required for
an active legal hold, litigation, or regulatory investigation, the business
unit that owns the record arranges for its deletion in accordance with the
company's IT standards.

---

## 4. Legal holds

Where a legal hold, litigation, or regulatory investigation applies, the
retention periods in this Schedule are suspended for the records in scope
until the hold is lifted.

---

## 5. Governance and Review

### 5.1 Accountability

The Head of Privacy owns this Schedule. Business-unit leaders are responsible
for applying the retention periods in their areas.

### 5.2 Review

This Schedule is reviewed at least annually. Changes require approval by the
Privacy Council and Legal.
