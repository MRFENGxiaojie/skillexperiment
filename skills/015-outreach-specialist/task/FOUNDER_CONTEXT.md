# Founder Context

## Company

**LabelWorks** — B2B data labeling and annotation services provider. We help
AI/ML teams build training datasets for computer vision, NLP, and document
processing. Headquartered in Ho Chi Minh City, with a distributed workforce of
~300 labelers across three delivery hubs (Vietnam, Philippines, India).

## Positioning

- **Core promise:** cut data labeling cost by roughly 40% with equal or better
  quality, via QA-layered workflows and domain-specialized labeler pools.
- Pricing is per-task or per-hour project-based; we compete on cost-to-quality
  ratio against US/EU suppliers and on process maturity against freelancer marketplaces.
- Industries we serve: e-commerce, fintech, healthcare (non-PHI), logistics.
- Certifications: ISO 27001 (in progress), SOC 2 Type II gap assessment done.
- Typical engagement: pilot on one labeling task, then scale to full project.

## Proof Case (use in outreach)

A mid-size SaaS company (annual revenue ~30M RMB) running a document-intake AI
feature came to us with out-of-control labeling spend:

- Before: ~24,000 RMB per month on labeling (mix of freelancers and an agency)
- After: ~15,000 RMB per month with us
- Time to impact: 6 weeks from kickoff to steady state
- Quality: their in-house acceptance rate stayed above 98%
