Master Service Agreement (signed in 2021)
Signature date: 2021-03-15 (original version, not amended)
Article 1 Definitions and Interpretation
1.1 "Services" means the software license and support services the Supplier provides to the Customer under this Agreement.
1.2 "Customer Data" means the data the Customer uploads into the Supplier's system.
Article 2 Scope of Services
2.1 The Supplier provides access to the SaaS platform AuroraSuite, including standard features and annual maintenance.
2.2 Service level: monthly availability of no less than 99.5%.
Article 3 Term
3.1 This Agreement takes effect on March 15, 2021, with a term of three years, and will automatically renew for one year upon expiry.
Article 5 Fees
5.1 Annual fee of CNY 480,000, payable annually in advance.
Article 10 Indemnification
10.1
If a third party claims that the Customer's use of the Services infringes its intellectual property rights, the Supplier shall defend at its own cost and compensate the Customer for the final judgment amounts and reasonable attorneys' fees incurred as a result.
10.2 The Customer shall be liable to compensate the Supplier for losses caused by the Customer's breach of confidentiality obligations or misuse of the Services.
10.3 The obligations under this Article shall remain in effect for three years after the termination of this Agreement.
Article 13 Termination
13.1 If either party commits a material breach and fails to cure it within 30 days after written notice, the non-breaching party may terminate this Agreement.
13.2 Upon termination, the Supplier shall return the Customer Data to the Customer and delete relevant copies.
