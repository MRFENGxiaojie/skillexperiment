Article 1 Lease Term
1.1 The lease term runs from September 1, 2024 to August 31, 2025.
1.2 The parties renewed the lease for one year on September 1, 2025 (until August 31, 2026), with the monthly rent adjusted to $1,450; all other terms remain unchanged.
Article 2 Rent and Security Deposit
2.1 Monthly rent is $1,450, payable on or before the 1st day of each month.
2.2 The security deposit is $1,450, to be returned without interest within 30 days after the lease term expires and the premises are free of damage.
Article 3 Facilities and Maintenance
3.1 Party A is responsible for the normal operation and repair of necessary facilities including heating, hot water, electricity, and plumbing.
3.2 Party A shall respond within a reasonable time after receiving a maintenance request (emergency matters affecting living conditions, such as heating and hot water, shall not exceed 72 hours).
Article 4 Habitability
4.1 Party A warrants that the premises meet habitability standards during the lease term, and the heating equipment shall keep the indoor temperature at no less than 18°C (65°F) during the heating season.
Article 5 Breach and Termination
5.1 If either party breaches the contract, it shall bear breach liability to the non-breaching party and compensate for losses.
5.2 If Party B delays rent payment for more than 5 days, Party A is entitled to issue a written demand; if payment is still not made within 7 days after the demand, Party A may terminate the contract.
5.3 Party B may terminate the lease early only by giving Party A 30 days' written notice.
Article 6 Material Breach by Party A
6.1 If Party A fails to maintain its obligations under Articles 3 and 4 (especially heating), and the issue is not repaired within 7 days after Party B's written notice, Party B is entitled to terminate the contract and claim damages.
Article 7 Notices
7.1 Written notices between the parties shall be delivered to the addresses specified in this contract by email or mail.
