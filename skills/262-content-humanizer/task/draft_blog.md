# Why Customer Service Teams Should Automate

In today's fast-paced digital era, the quality and efficiency of customer service have become core elements of a company's competitiveness. Customer service teams face unprecedented challenges and opportunities, and how to improve their work efficiency is an important topic that every business manager needs to think about seriously.

## The real predicament customer service teams are facing

As everyone knows, customer service teams have to handle a large number of repetitive inquiries every day. According to industry reports, customer service teams handle about 60% repetitive inquiries on average every day. These repetitive questions not only take up the valuable time and energy of service staff, but also seriously affect their focus and motivation for handling complex problems and delivering high-quality service.

It is worth noting that as business scale keeps growing, the workload of customer service teams is also increasing. If effective measures are not taken, customer service teams will fall into the double predicament of "never-ending repetitive inquiries" and "inability to improve service quality".

## How automation empowers customer service teams

First, automation can greatly improve the response speed of customer service teams. After an e-commerce platform implemented customer-service automation, the average first response time dropped from 4 hours to 20 minutes, and customer satisfaction (CSAT) improved by 12 percentage points. This significant result fully proves the important value of automation in the customer-service field.

Second, automation can effectively reduce the operating costs of customer service teams. After a SaaS company handed 70% of repetitive tickets to an automated ticketing system in 2023, the customer service team shrank from 15 people to 9, while NPS rose from 38 to 52. This not only reduced costs and improved efficiency, but also enhanced the overall customer experience.

Third, automation can deliver 7x24 uninterrupted service. No matter when or where, the automation system can provide customers with immediate responses and solutions, completely solving the pain point of "no one answers outside working hours" in the traditional customer-service model.

## Best practices for automation

When promoting customer-service automation, companies need to follow these best practices:

First, choose the right use cases. Not every customer-service process suits automation; companies should prioritize high-frequency, repetitive, rule-clear inquiry scenarios for automation.

Second, build a complete automation knowledge base. A high-quality knowledge base is the key foundation for successful customer-service automation; companies need to continuously update and optimize the knowledge-base content.

Third, value human-machine collaboration. Automation is not meant to replace human agents, but to free them from repetitive labor so they can focus on more complex, more valuable customer service.

## In conclusion

In conclusion, customer-service automation is an irreversible trend in the wave of digital transformation. It can not only significantly improve a company's operating efficiency and customer satisfaction, but also help the company win the first-mover advantage in fierce market competition.
