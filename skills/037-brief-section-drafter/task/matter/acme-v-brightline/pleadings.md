# PLEADINGS SUMMARY — Acme v. Brightline

> Summaries for drafting context. Cite the docket entry, not this summary, in the brief.

## Complaint (filed 2026-07-10, Dkt. 1 — N.D. Tex.)

Counts:
1. **Breach of contract** — Brightline failed to pay invoices 10258, 10264, 10279
   ($48,500 total) when due under Supply Agreement §4.2; demand made; unpaid.
2. **Account stated** — invoices retained without objection; balance stated and unpaid.

Relief sought: $48,500 + interest under §4.3 + costs; such further relief as the court deems just.

## Answer and Affirmative Defenses (filed 2026-07-24, Dkt. 8)

- Admits: contract existence (2025-12-01), invoices issued, invoices unpaid.
- Denies: that payment was due when alleged; that amounts are owed.
- **Affirmative Defense 3 (Modification):** "The parties mutually agreed in February
  2026, by email and course of conduct, to extend Plaintiff's payment terms to sixty
  (60) days from invoice. Defendant has paid and will continue to pay in accordance
  with the modified terms."
- **Affirmative Defense 4 (Estoppel):** "Plaintiff is estopped from denying the
  modification by its representations of flexibility in February 2026, on which
  Defendant relied in ordering additional volume."
- **Affirmative Defense 7 (Breach/withholding):** "Plaintiff breached by withholding
  shipment under order BL-8842 without notice or right."
- No counterclaim was filed.

February 2026 email exchange cannot, as a matter of law, have modified §4.2 in light
of §7.2's signed-writing requirement and §7.3's no-authority clause, and that no
genuine dispute of material fact exists. Brightline's modification defense is the
