# SUPPLY AGREEMENT — Acme Manufacturing, Inc. & Brightline Distribution, LLC
# (relevant excerpts; full agreement AG-0001, produced in this matter)

**Effective date:** 2025-12-01
**Parties:** Acme Manufacturing, Inc. ("Supplier") and Brightline Distribution, LLC ("Buyer")
**Goods:** industrial fasteners, components, and related hardware per Buyer purchase orders
**Term:** one year, renewing automatically unless terminated on 60 days' notice

## § 4. Payment

**§4.1** Supplier shall invoice each shipment upon dispatch. Each invoice shall state
the purchase order number, quantity, unit price, and date of shipment.

**§4.2** Buyer shall pay each invoice in full within thirty (30) days of the invoice
date. Payment shall be made by wire transfer or ACH to the account designated by
Supplier. Late payments shall accrue interest as set forth in §4.3.

**§4.3** Past-due amounts shall accrue interest at the lesser of one and one-half
percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by applicable law, from the
due date until paid.

**§4.4** Time is of the essence with respect to Buyer's payment obligations. No course
of dealing, trade practice, or oral representation shall vary the payment terms of
this Agreement.

## § 7. Amendments and Waivers

**§7.1** Either party may waive a provision of this Agreement only by a writing signed
by the waiving party, and no waiver of any breach shall be deemed a waiver of any
other or subsequent breach.

**§7.2** No amendment or modification of this Agreement shall be effective unless in
writing and signed by authorized representatives of both parties. Purchase orders,
invoices, emails, and other correspondence shall not constitute amendments to this
Agreement. [THE CENTRAL PROVISION FOR THE MODIFICATION ARGUMENT]

**§7.3** The parties acknowledge that employees and sales personnel have no authority
to modify this Agreement or any of its terms.

## § 9. Termination and Cure

**§9.3** If either party fails to perform any material obligation, the other party may
provide written notice and, if the failure is not cured within fourteen (14) days,
treat the failure as a material breach. Nothing in this section limits remedies for
non-payment.

## § 13. Governing Law and Venue

**§13.1** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed under the laws of the
State of Texas, without regard to conflict-of-laws principles.
**§13.2** The parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the federal and state
courts in Dallas County, Texas.
