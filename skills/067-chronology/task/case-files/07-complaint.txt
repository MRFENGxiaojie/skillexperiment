COMPLAINT FOR BREACH OF CONTRACT

1. Plaintiff Acme Corp is a Delaware corporation with principal place of business in San Jose, California.
2. Defendant OurCompany, Inc. is a California corporation with principal place of business in Palo Alto, California.
3. On March 15, 2024, Plaintiff and Defendant entered into a Supply Agreement for industrial fasteners.
4. Defendant failed to pay invoices totaling $35,625 for Q4 2024 and Q1 2025 deliveries.
5. Defendant's payment delays constitute breach of contract under §4.2 of the Agreement.
6. Plaintiff seeks: (a) $35,625 in unpaid invoices, (b) $5,344 in late payment interest per §4.3, (c) declaratory judgment that Defendant's default claims are without merit, (d) attorneys' fees and costs.
