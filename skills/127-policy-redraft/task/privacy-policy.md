This is the current official version of the company's Personal Information
Protection Policy. It applies to all business units, subsidiaries, and
employees of the company, and to any third party that processes personal
information on the company's behalf.

---

## 1. Purpose and Scope

### 1.1 Purpose

The purpose of this Policy is to protect the personal information the company
collects, uses, stores, and shares in the course of its business, and to
ensure that such information is handled lawfully, fairly, and transparently.

### 1.2 Scope

This Policy applies to all personal information handled by the company,
regardless of the medium on which the information is stored. It covers
information about customers, employees, prospective employees, contractors,
suppliers, and business contacts. Each business unit is responsible for
implementing this Policy within its area of responsibility.

### 1.3 Relationship to other policies

Where a local law or another company policy imposes a higher standard of
protection than this Policy, the higher standard applies.

---

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Personal Information

For the purposes of this Policy, **Personal Information** means any information
that directly identifies an individual natural person, including but not
limited to:

- full name;
- government-issued identifiers (for example, Social Security number, passport
  number, or driver's license number);
- direct contact details (for example, home address, personal telephone number,
  or personal email address).

Personal Information does **not** include:

- information that has been aggregated or de-identified so that it can no
  longer be linked to an identifiable individual;
- pseudonymized data (data from which direct identifiers have been removed,
  even where a key could re-associate the data with an individual);
- any information that is not stored in a structured system, including
  information captured solely in free-form documents, emails, or unstructured
  files; or
- information relating to a legal entity rather than a natural person.

### 2.2 Personal Information Breach

**Personal Information Breach** means the accidental or unlawful destruction,
loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of, or access to, personal
information.

### 2.3 Processing

**Processing** means any operation performed on personal information, including
collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use,
disclosure, or erasure.

---

## 3. Collection Principles

### 3.1 Lawful and fair collection

The company collects personal information only for legitimate business
purposes and only to the extent necessary for those purposes. Collection is
transparent: individuals are informed at the point of collection of what is
collected, why, and how long it will be kept.

### 3.2 Consent

Where consent is required by law, the company obtains consent in a manner that
is freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous. Consent may be withdrawn
at any time, and withdrawal is as easy as giving consent.

### 3.3 Data minimization

The company limits collection to the personal information that is adequate,
relevant, and necessary for the stated purpose. Personal information is not
collected on the basis of potential future, unspecified uses.

---

## 4. Use and Disclosure

### 4.1 Permitted uses

The company uses personal information only for the purposes for which it was
collected, or for compatible purposes consistent with the original notice.

### 4.2 Disclosure to third parties

The company discloses personal information to third parties only where a
lawful basis exists and where the recipient is bound by appropriate safeguards
(including a written data-processing agreement). Service providers may process
personal information only on documented instructions from the company.

### 4.3 Cross-border transfers

Where personal information is transferred to a country or international
organization outside the individual's jurisdiction, the company ensures
appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable law.

---

## 5. Data Retention

### 5.1 Retention periods

The company retains personal information only for as long as necessary to
fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by law. Each
business unit maintains a records-retention schedule documenting applicable
retention periods.

### 5.2 Secure deletion

When personal information is no longer needed, it is securely deleted,
destroyed, or rendered permanently unusable. Paper records are shredded;
electronic records are securely erased in accordance with the company's IT
standards.

---

## 6. Individual Rights

### 6.1 Rights of individuals

The company honors all rights granted to individuals under applicable law,
including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to
processing of their personal information, and the right to data portability.

### 6.2 Requests

Requests to exercise these rights are handled through the Privacy Office.
The company responds to verified requests without undue delay and in no case
later than the deadline required by applicable law.

---

## 7. Security Safeguards

### 7.1 Technical and organizational measures

The company implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to
protect personal information against unauthorized or unlawful processing and
against accidental loss, destruction, or damage. Measures include access
controls, encryption, employee training, and incident-response procedures.

### 7.2 Breach response

Suspected Personal Information Breaches are reported immediately to the
Privacy Office, which coordinates assessment, containment, and any required
notification in accordance with this Policy and applicable law.

---

## 8. Governance and Review

### 8.1 Accountability

The Head of Privacy owns this Policy and is accountable for its
implementation. Business-unit leaders are responsible for compliance within
their units.

### 8.2 Review

This Policy is reviewed at least annually and whenever there is a material
change in applicable law or in the company's processing activities. Changes
to this Policy require approval by the Privacy Council and Legal.
