## Background
Our team wants to build a bookkeeping and invoicing tool for freelancers. We have an initial idea but haven't written a line of code. Before launch we want to confirm whether this need actually exists.

## Task
Help us design a round of user research. We want to know: how freelancers currently keep books, what their biggest pain points are, and whether they'd pay for an automatic-invoicing feature. We plan to interview about 10 freelancers and run an online survey at scale (planning to send it to 200 people). Please provide: clear research objectives and hypotheses to test; how to screen interview participants (including screening questions); a complete interview guide where questions dig for real past events rather than vague "would you use this" questions; the survey questions; and how to analyze results — how to code and theme interview notes, and how to tabulate the survey. Also leave a structure for recording findings so we can capture evidence, surprises, confidence, and limitations. Write all output in English.
