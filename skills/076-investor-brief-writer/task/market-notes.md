# Market Notes — Market Size Estimation Methodology and Sources

## Target Population (China)
- College students enrolled (undergraduate + associate degree): about 47 million (per the definition in the Ministry of Education's "2025 Statistical Bulletin on the Development of National Education")
- Graduate students: about 3.8 million (same source)
- Total student base: about 50 million (**estimated**)

## TAM (Total Addressable Market)
- Assumption: 60% of students use study/note tools during the semester (research institutions report a range of 40%-70%; the midpoint is taken)
- Assumption: the average annual amount students spend from disposable income on study tools is CNY 100-300 (benchmarked against the pricing of similar subscription products, **estimated**)
- TAM ≈ 50 million x 60% x CNY 200/year ≈ CNY 6 billion/year (**estimated**)

## SAM (Serviceable Available Market)
- Excluding students without smartphones or a stable network (about 15%, **estimated**)
- SAM ≈ CNY 6 billion x 85% ≈ CNY 5.1 billion/year (**estimated**)

## SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market, 3-5 years)
- Assuming penetration of 0.5%-1% of the student population: 250K-500K paying users
- Unit price of CNY 200/year: SOM ≈ CNY 50M - 100M/year (**estimated**)

## Benchmark References
- Evernote China version (reference for learning-scenario penetration), GoodNotes, Notability priced at CNY 68-88/year
- Domestic online education SaaS companies (e.g., mind-map and vocabulary apps) have paying conversion rates of 1%-3% (**estimated**)
- Reference reports: iResearch "China Online Education Industry Report 2025", 多鲸 "Annual Report on Education Technology Investment and Financing 2025"
