# 记账鸭 app product info

## Product overview
- Product: 记账鸭 v2.1
- Type: personal accounting app, iOS/Android
- Core features: quick expense logging, bill reports, budget reminders
- Live for six months; App Store / app marketplace rating 3.8

## Core flow
"Open → pick category → enter amount → save"; logging one entry takes 6 steps on average.

## Operations data
- 40K monthly active users
- Next-day retention 32%
- Team goal: improve next-day retention and rating

## User feedback excerpts
- "Too many taps to log one entry"
- "Can't understand the charts"
- "Don't know where to switch ledgers"
- "It crashed once; no data was lost, but it was alarming"

## Negative-review hotspots
1. The logging flow is too cumbersome
2. Can't find the settings entry
3. Reports are hard to understand
