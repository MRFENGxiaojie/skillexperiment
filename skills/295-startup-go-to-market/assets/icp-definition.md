# ICP Definition Template

Use this template to draft the ICP during Workflow step 1. Keep it to one page; if it runs longer, you are describing two ICPs and should split.

## Primary ICP

- **Who**: [title / role / team]
- **Firmographics**: [size, industry, geography]
- **Pain**: [top 3 symptoms, in the customer's words]
- **Current alternative**: [what they use today to solve this]
- **Procurement**: [budget owner, approval path, contract size]
- **Trigger events**: [what starts the search — hires, incidents, vendor expirations, growth milestones]
- **Activation**: [what "first value" looks like in the first session or week]

## Secondary ICP

[Same fields, one line per field. Include only if it materially changes targeting or messaging.]

## Anti-ICP

[Who to exclude: wrong segment, no budget, heavy support load, no expansion path.]

## Buying path

1. [Who recognizes the problem]
2. [Who evaluates]
3. [Who champions]
4. [Who decides]
5. [Who pays]

## Tiering rules

- **High fit + high intent**: prioritized outreach / PQL routing
- **High fit + low intent**: nurture with content
- **Low fit**: out of scope even if inbound
