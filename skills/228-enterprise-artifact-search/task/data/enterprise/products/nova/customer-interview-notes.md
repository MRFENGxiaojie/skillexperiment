## Interview Overview

Interviews with 5 education institution customers, 9 interviewees in total, centered on course management processes and renewal decisions.

## Key Points

- Customers generally make renewal decisions at the "end of trial period" milestone.
- The interview outline structure (background → process → pain points → decision) can serve as a reference for other product lines; the CoachForce research has cited this outline.
