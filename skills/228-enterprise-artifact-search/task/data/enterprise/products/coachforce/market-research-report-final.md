## 1. Research Background and Sample

A dedicated questionnaire survey for the CoachForce quarterly review. After expanding the sample per the review comments, the sample size is 1800, with a 95% confidence interval of ±2.3%; the sample description is in Appendix A.

## 2. Competitor Comparison

Compared against the feature matrices of competitors Atlas, Nova, and overseas similar products. The channel structure section has been supplemented with raw data on each agency channel's revenue share ratios (see Appendix C), on the basis of which Conclusion 4 holds.

## 3. Main Conclusions

- Conclusion 1: Assessment automation is the biggest demand point (priority P0).
- Conclusion 2: The frequency of team feedback tracking usage is lower than expected (P1).
- Conclusion 3: Pricing sensitivity — per the corrected wording/definition (excluding churned customers with no active records in the last 90 days), paid conversion is not sensitive to price, and the current pricing can be maintained (pricing experiment in Appendix B).
- Conclusion 4: In the channel structure, agencies contribute about 60% of new signings, and differences in revenue share ratios are the main variable in commercial negotiations.
