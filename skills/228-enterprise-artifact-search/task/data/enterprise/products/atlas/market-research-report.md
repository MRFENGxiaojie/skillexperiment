## 1. Sample

In-depth interviews with 30 Atlas enterprise-edition users + 600 online questionnaires.

## 2. Key Findings

- Driver of existing customer renewals: the smoothness of data import.
- Main cause of new customer churn: onboarding cost and insufficient number of templates.

## 3. Conclusions

The key investment direction on the Atlas side is data migration tools and expansion of the template library, which is unrelated to CoachForce's channel and pricing topics.
