# Aurora Analytics, Inc. — Shareholders Agreement

- Date: 2019-04-20
- Parties: Founders (D. Lin, M. Okafor, S. Patel) and employees holding options
- Key provisions:
  - Right of first refusal on transfers (company + other shareholders)
  - Drag-along: holders of 2/3 of common may drag on a qualifying sale
  - Tag-along: any founder sale triggers tag rights
  - No forum selection clause — disputes under the agreement subject to Delaware Chancery
- **Gap:** No written consent requirement for a sale of all or substantially all assets noted in this agreement — check bylaws for asset-sale approval mechanics.
