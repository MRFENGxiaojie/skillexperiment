# Contract 01 — Northwind Health (Major Client) — $2,000,000/yr

- **Counterparty:** Northwind Health Systems, Inc.
- **Type:** Master SaaS agreement (data analytics platform)
- **Annual value:** $2,000,000
- **Term:** 2024-01-01 — 2027-12-31 (3 yr + auto-renew 1 yr)
- **Key terms:**
  - **Change of control:** Section 12.1 — Northwind may terminate for convenience within 30 days of any change of control of Aurora, without penalty. 
  - **Assignment:** Aurora may not assign without prior written consent (Section 12.3); consent may not be unreasonably withheld.
  - **Exclusivity:** None.
  - **Indemnity:** Aurora indemnifies Northwind for all claims "arising from or relating to the Platform or any service", including claims of third-party IP infringement — broad, no cap on IP infringement indemnity; confidentiality/data breach indemnity uncapped.
  - **Limitation of liability:** capped at 12 months' fees; **exceptions carve out the uncapped indemnities above and data breach.**
  - **SLA:** 99.9% uptime; service credits up to 10%.
- **Status:** Executed, in force. Renewal notice window: 90 days before 2027-12-31.
