# Privacy policy (public-facing version, summary)

- Collection: resume text (name, contact details, work history, education, etc.)
- Use: hiring screening and scoring only
- Storage: cloud service, Europe (Frankfurt data center)
- Retention: resume data kept for 12 months after the recruitment process ends (see also the data-retention policy)
- Rights: candidates may request access, correction, or deletion of their resume data
- Third parties: model inference is processed by a third-party API provider

(Note: this file is one of the existing documents and has not been formally reviewed by the legal department.)
