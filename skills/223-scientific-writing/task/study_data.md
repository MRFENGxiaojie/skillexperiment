# Study basic information (input for the paper to be written)

## Design

- Single-center retrospective cohort study.
- Enrollment period: January 2019 – December 2023.
- Study population: adult patients with type 2 diabetes, **1,847 cases** in total.

## Groups (Exposure)

- Exposure group: SGLT2 inhibitors, **n = 612**.
- Control group: other oral hypoglycemic drugs, **n = 1,235**.

## Outcomes

- Primary outcome: **MACE** (composite outcome of cardiovascular death / myocardial infarction / stroke).
- Secondary outcomes: not provided (may be left blank and ask the PI).

## Follow-up

- Median follow-up **3.2 years**.

## Statistical results (already calculated; use directly)

| Metric | SGLT2 group | Control group |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MACE event rate | 6.9% | 9.4% |
| Adjusted HR | 0.72 (95% CI 0.58–0.89) | 1.00 (reference) |

- Post-PSM sensitivity analysis: HR = 0.74 (95% CI 0.60–0.91), results consistent.
